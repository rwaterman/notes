# Stuck in a Rut?

Four questions for getting unstuck and back into motion.

> [!quote] Graham Weaver
> Momentum beats planning. Translate the goal into something small you can do *today*, then repeat.

1. **What am I avoiding?** — Go straight at it. The thing you're avoiding is usually the thing that matters.
2. **Where do I start?** — Translate the goal into something very simple you can do today.
3. **How do I win today?** — Write down three things you can move forward on today. Do them, then repeat tomorrow.
4. **What habits are interfering?** — Name the patterns pulling you away from where you want to go.
